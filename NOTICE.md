# Provenance

OpenBB was started by Trass3r. This fork retains the original copyright and
GPL-3.0-or-later notice in LICENSE and the upstream Git history. The cleanup
baseline is upstream commit `aedc3fcf2e27caad6005e5d1a0459e380a04b27f`.

The inherited M10/PT parser credits Valery V. Anisimovsky
(`samael@avn.mccme.ru`) for header-reading code. This attribution is retained as
the parser is bounded and modernized. The speech codec, palettes and PNG writer
are inherited OpenBB work; this milestone does not claim independent authorship
of their algorithms or data.

Corpus hashes and format checks use our separate Beasts & Bumpkins investigation,
including BBTools comparisons. No original game payloads are distributed here.
The locally downloaded D compiler remains under its own bundled notices/licenses
and is excluded from Git.
